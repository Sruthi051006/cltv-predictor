Customer Lifetime Value Predictor

An explainable machine-learning system that predicts how much value each insurance customer will bring, assigns a value segment and tells you why - built with XGBoost and SHAP for Hackathon Challenge 50.

How it works

One pipeline runs identically in training and in the app, so raw customer data can be used directly.

Raw customerProfile, tenure, claim amount, number of policies.
Clean & capFix types and text, IQR capping of extreme values.
Feature engineeringClaim per year, multi-policy flag, interactions.
XGBoostTuned gradient-boosted trees predict CLTV.
DecisionValue segment, confidence band and SHAP factors.

Value segments and actions

Customers are grouped by predicted lifetime value.

Low

Serve through low-cost digital channels; offer a bundled second policy; avoid expensive retention spend.

Medium

Grow the relationship: cross-sell a second policy or upgrade the cover.

High

Protect: renewal benefits, a dedicated relationship manager, early-renewal discounts.
Dataset: insurance customer CLTV data (89,392 training customers). Model: XGBoost regression with an sklearn preprocessing pipeline. Predictions are estimates with an 80% confidence band, not guarantees.
